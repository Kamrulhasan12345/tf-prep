RB and AVL Gauntlet

Full exam runs, a one-page case table, the mistakes that lose marks, and mixed practice. 2024–25 Q5 was compulsory: 25 marks of red-black insert and delete plus 10 marks of AVL, 35 marks from this topic alone. Do the timed drill on paper before you open any step.

1. Timed drill: 2024–25 Q5(a), 25 minutes

"Starting from an empty red-black tree, insert 41, 38, 31, 12, 19, 8, 25, 32 in this order. Using the resulting tree, delete 8, 12 and 19 in that order. Show the tree after each operation, including the colour of every node." (25 marks)

Budget: eight inserts at about 1.5 minutes (12 minutes), three deletes at about 3 minutes (9 minutes), 4 minutes to check colours and black counts. 11 trees in total.

25:00

Method · how to run the drill

  1. Start the timer. Draw on paper only. Keep this page below the fold.
  2. After each operation, name the case aloud ("uncle red, case 1") before you draw.
  3. When time ends, compare tree by tree with the list below. Mark the first tree that differs, then use the stepper to see which case you misread.
  4. Do it a second time without the timer, fixing the cases you missed.

AVL (bf = height of left minus height of right)

Lowest unbalanced AHeavy child BCaseDo

AVL insertion stops after the first rotation. AVL deletion continues up to the root.

3. Common errors, all of them

Where marks go

  • Colours missing. If your trees do not show every colour, you lose marks even if the shape is right.
  • Uncle NIL treated as "no uncle". NIL is black. It selects case 2 or 3, not case 1.
  • Case 2 stopping early. After the bend rotation you must still apply case 3.
  • Case 1 without a re-check. z moves to the grandparent. That node may now have a red parent.
  • Root left red. Recolour the root black at the end.
  • Deleting a red node and then "fixing". Red removal needs no fix.
  • Using the key's colour instead of the successor's original colour. The fix-up depends on the colour of the node that moved.
  • Successor forgets to take the deleted node's colour.
  • Mirror errors in deletion when x is the right child.
  • Case 1 and case 3 finished as if complete. Both continue.
  • AVL: sign mix-up. Write "bf = h(left) − h(right)" first.
  • AVL: single rotation for an LR or RL case. A zig-zag needs two rotations.
  • AVL deletion: stopping after one rotation. Keep walking to the root.
  • AVL: rotating at the wrong node. Double rotation first rotates at the child B, then at A. One student solution to the 2023–24 paper rotated the wrong node.
