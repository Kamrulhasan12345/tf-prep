Circuits with Several Transistors

Two-transistor DC questions carried 35 marks in 2022–23 (a differential pair and an npn–pnp pair), 20⅔ in 2020–21 (complementary follower) and 30⅔ in 2017–18 (Sedra's two-stage circuit). Nothing new is needed beyond Chapter 3: decide which transistors conduct, then write exact KCL at the node where they meet, including the base current the second transistor draws. Sadat sir explicitly prefers this exact method to the textbook's "ignore \(I_{B2}\)" shortcut.

1. The general method

Method · multi-transistor DC (type B-03)

  1. Decide which transistors can conduct. Two complementary devices with tied bases and tied emitters can never both be on: npn needs \(V_B - V_E \ge 0.7\), pnp needs \(V_E - V_B \ge 0.7\).
  2. Solve the stage whose bias does not depend on the other (usually the first: Thevenin + base loop).
  3. At the node joining the stages write KCL with the second base current: e.g. \(I_{RC1} + I_{B2} = I_{C1}\) for an npn collector driving a pnp base.
  4. Write the second transistor's base–emitter loop with \(I_{E2} = (\beta_2+1)I_{B2}\) and solve the two equations together.
  5. Check the mode of every transistor, and that an "off" device really has \(|V_{BE}| < 0.7\) V.

2. Complementary (push–pull) follower

Three cases. \(v_i > 0.7\) V: Q1 on, Q2 off, \(I_B = (v_i - 0.7)/(R_B + (\beta+1)R_L)\), \(v_o = (\beta+1)I_BR_L\). \(v_i < -0.7\) V: Q2 on, current flows up from ground through \(R_L\) into Q2's emitter, so \(v_o = -(\beta+1)I_BR_L\) with \(I_B = (-v_i - 0.7)/(R_B + (\beta+1)R_L)\). \(|v_i| < 0.7\) V: both off, \(v_o = 0\) (the dead zone). Sadat sir's cases, recomputed (10 kΩ, 1 kΩ, β = 100): \(v_i\) = 5, 2, 0.5, −1, −3 V give \(v_o\) = 3.913, 1.183, 0, −0.273, −2.093 V. (The class notes write the last two as positive: a sign slip.)

3. Differential pair

Matched transistors with tied emitters fed by a tail current \(I\) and equal base voltages share the tail equally: \(I_{E1} = I_{E2} = I/2\), \(I_C = \alpha I/2\), \(V_C = V_{CC} - \alpha(I/2)R_C\), \(V_E = V_B - 0.7\). If the tail is a resistor \(R_E\) to \(-V_{EE}\) instead of a current source, \(I = (V_B - 0.7 + V_{EE})/R_E\). Check \(V_{CE} \ge 0.3\) V.

4. npn stage driving a pnp stage

Method · npn → pnp, exact

  1. Q1: Thevenin \(V_{BB}\), \(R_B\); \(I_{B1} = (V_{BB} - 0.7)/(R_B + (\beta_1+1)R_{E1})\), \(I_{C1} = \beta_1I_{B1}\).
  2. KVL from \(V_{CC}\) down \(R_{E2}\), across E–B of Q2 and back up \(R_{C1}\): \(R_{C1}(I_{C1} - I_{B2}) = 0.7 + (\beta_2+1)I_{B2}R_{E2}\).
  3. So \(I_{B2} = \dfrac{R_{C1}I_{C1} - 0.7}{R_{C1} + (\beta_2+1)R_{E2}}\); then \(I_{C2} = \beta_2I_{B2}\), \(V_{C1} = V_{CC} - R_{C1}(I_{C1} - I_{B2})\), \(V_{E2} = V_{C1} + 0.7\), \(V_{C2} = I_{C2}R_{C2}\).
  4. Check \(V_{CE1} \ge 0.3\) and \(V_{EC2} \ge 0.3\).

Worked example: 2017–18 Sec A Q3(b) (β = 100 assumed; not printed on the paper)

Traps (where marks go)

  • Ignoring \(I_{B2}\) at the joining node. Sedra's first pass does this (\(V_{C1}\) = 8.6 V) and then corrects it (8.75 V); with a large \(R_{C1}\) or small β the error is bigger.
  • Mixing \(\beta_n\) and \(\beta_p\) (2022–23 gives 120 and 80).
  • Assuming both complementary transistors conduct; or forgetting the minus sign of \(v_o\) when the pnp conducts.
  • Assuming β = ∞ when β is not given: in 2017–18 that makes Q2 saturate (\(V_{C2}\) = 8.73 V > \(V_{E2}\) = 8.53 V), so state a finite β (100).

Exam questions

2022–23 · Sec B · Q6(b) · 20 marks

\(\beta_n\) = 120 for the npn and \(\beta_p\) = 80 for the pnp. Determine \(I_{B1}, I_{C1}, I_{B2}, I_{C2}, V_{CE1}, V_{EC2}\). \(V^+\) = 9 V; 80 kΩ from \(V^+\) to \(B_1\), 40 kΩ from \(B_1\) to ground; 2 kΩ from \(V^+\) to \(C_1\), 2 kΩ from \(E_1\) to ground; pnp base at \(C_1\), 100 Ω from \(V^+\) to \(E_2\), 200 Ω from \(C_2\) (= \(V_O\)) to ground.

Show worked answer

Q1: \(V_{BB} = 9\times40/120 = 3\) V, \(R_B = 80\parallel40 = 26.67\) kΩ. \(I_{B1} = \dfrac{3 - 0.7}{26.67 + 121\times2} = 8.561\) µA; \(I_{C1} = 1.027\) mA; \(I_{E1} = 1.036\) mA.

Q2: KCL at \(C_1\): \((9 - V_{C1})/2 + I_{B2} = I_{C1}\), and \(V_{C1} = 9 - 0.7 - 81\times0.1\,I_{B2}\). Substituting: \((0.7 + 8.1I_{B2})/2 + I_{B2} = 1.027\) → \(I_{B2} = (1.027 - 0.35)/5.05 = 0.1341\) mA. \(I_{C2} = 80I_{B2} = 10.73\) mA, \(I_{E2}\) = 10.86 mA.

Voltages: \(V_{C1} = 8.3 - 8.1\times0.1341 = 7.214\) V, \(V_{E1} = 2\times1.036 = 2.072\) V, \(V_{CE1}\) = 5.142 V. \(V_{E2} = 9 - 0.1\times10.86 = 7.914\) V, \(V_O = 0.2\times10.73 = 2.146\) V, \(V_{EC2}\) = 5.768 V. Both active. (The senior "BJT TF Analysis" file rounds \(I_{B2}\) to 0.13 mA and reports \(I_{C2}\) = 10.4 mA, \(V_{EC2}\) = 5.867 V: rounding slips.)

2022–23 · Sec B · Q6(a) · 15 marks

Differential pair: both bases grounded, 4 kΩ from +5 V to each collector, a 1 mA current source from the joined emitters to −5 V. β = 200 for each transistor. Find (i) \(I_{E1}\), (ii) \(I_{E2}\), (iii) \(V_{C1}\), (iv) \(V_{C2}\).

Show worked answer

Matched, equal base voltages: \(I_{E1} = I_{E2} = 0.5\) mA. \(\alpha = 200/201 = 0.995\), \(I_C = 0.4975\) mA. \(V_{C1} = V_{C2} = 5 - 4\times0.4975 = 3.01\) V. \(V_E = -0.7\) V, \(V_{CE}\) = 3.71 V: active. (The senior file writes 3.04 V from \(I_C\) = 0.49 mA.)

2020–21 · Sec A · Q2(b) · 20⅔ marks  |  = Sedra Example 6.13 · Sadat L13, notes p.54–57

Find the voltages at all nodes and the currents through all branches. β = 100. (Figure 1 with \(v_i\) = +5 V, \(R_B\) = 10 kΩ, \(R_L\) = 1 kΩ, rails ±5 V.)

Show worked answer

Both cannot conduct. With +5 V at the input, try Q1 on, Q2 off: \(I_B = (5 - 0.7)/(10 + 101\times1) = 38.74\) µA, \(I_C = 3.874\) mA, \(I_E = 3.913\) mA = \(I_{RL}\).

\(V_B = 5 - 10\times0.03874 = 4.613\) V, \(V_E = 3.913\) V, \(V_C = 5\) V. Q2: \(V_{EB2} = V_E - V_B = -0.7\) V, so it is off (all its currents 0). Q1: \(V_{CE} = 1.087\) V ≥ 0.3: active. Current in 10 kΩ = \(I_B\).

2017–18 · Sec A · Q3(b) · 30⅔ marks  |  = Sedra Example 6.12 · Sadat L12, notes p.53 · Khairul s.25

Determine \(I_{C1}\) and \(I_{C2}\) for the circuit of Figure 2 (+15 V; 100 kΩ/50 kΩ divider, \(R_{C1}\) = 5 kΩ, \(R_{E1}\) = 3 kΩ; pnp with \(R_{E2}\) = 2 kΩ to +15 V and \(R_{C2}\) = 2.7 kΩ to ground). β is not printed; take β = 100.

Show worked answer

Q1 (Chapter 4): \(I_{B1}\) = 12.78 µA, \(I_{C1}\) = 1.278 mA, \(V_{E1}\) = 3.874 V.

Q2: \(5(1.278 - I_{B2}) = 0.7 + 2\times101I_{B2}\) → \(I_{B2} = (6.392 - 0.7)/207 = 27.50\) µA; \(I_{C2}\) = 2.750 mA, \(I_{E2}\) = 2.777 mA.

\(V_{C1} = 15 - 5(1.278 - 0.0275) = 8.745\) V; \(V_{E2}\) = 9.445 V; \(V_{C2} = 2.7\times2.750 = 7.425\) V. Checks: \(V_{CE1}\) = 4.87 V, \(V_{EC2}\) = 2.02 V: both active.

\(\beta_{min}\) = 50; assume saturation. Q3 pnp: 10 kΩ from +5 V to the emitter, base 10 kΩ to ground, collector 20 kΩ to ground and also driving Q4's base. Q4 npn: 30 kΩ from +5 V to the collector, 10 kΩ from the emitter to ground (read from the figure). Find \(V_{C3}\), \(V_{C4}\) and the forced betas.

Unknown \(V_{B3}\): \(V_{E3} = V_{B3} + 0.7\), \(V_{C3} = V_{E3} - 0.2\), \(V_{E4} = V_{C3} - 0.7\), \(V_{C4} = V_{E4} + 0.2\); \(I_{B4} = V_{E4}/10 - (5 - V_{C4})/30\); KCL at \(C_3\): \(I_{C3} = V_{C3}/20 + I_{B4}\); and \(I_{E3} = I_{B3} + I_{C3}\). Key terms

Quick check

Summary: decide who conducts (complementary pairs: only one; dead zone \(|v_i| < 0.7\) V), solve the independent stage first, then write exact KCL at the joining node with the second base current: \(I_{B2} = (R_{C1}I_{C1} - 0.7)/(R_{C1} + (\beta_2+1)R_{E2})\). A matched pair splits the tail current equally. Check every transistor's mode at the end.
