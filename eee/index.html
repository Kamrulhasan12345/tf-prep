What the paper looks like now

Since 2022–23: 210 marks, 3 hours, two sections of four 35-mark questions. Q1 and Q5 are compulsory; answer any two others in each section. One section is usually "devices" (diodes, BJT, MOSFET) and the other "linear ICs" (op-amps, oscillators, 555), but they swap between years, so prepare both. Almost every question is a Sedra & Smith example or exercise with new numbers. Details: Exam format and strategy.

How to use this book

  • Contents: the sidebar on the left (the Contents menu on a phone).
  • Method boxes give the recipe for a problem type. Worked examples reveal one line at a time; write your guess for each line before revealing it.
  • Practice boxes generate new numbers every time. Type your answers (a bare number in the unit shown, or with a prefix like 4.7k or 2.2 mA). Guided steps lets you fill in each intermediate value. Three correct in a row without the solution marks the type mastered ★; progress is saved in this browser.
  • Open in simulator ↗ under a circuit opens it, already built and running, in the free Falstad circuit simulator: watch currents flow, hover to read voltages, change values, add a scope.
  • Exam questions at the end of each chapter are past term-final questions with full worked answers. Several circulating senior solutions and the AI-written question bank contain wrong answers; the answers here were recomputed, and the errors are noted where they matter.
