What the exam asks

Built from the three MATH 241 term finals (2022–23, 2023–24, 2024–25) plus the older MATH 147/143/245 papers, the class notes of all three teachers, and their slides.

Format: 210 marks, 3 hours. Section A = Q1–Q4, Section B = Q5–Q8, answer any 3 in each section, separate scripts. About 35 marks per question, so about 30 minutes each.

Questions that came back word for word

  • Green verify \((3x^2-8y^2)dx+(4y-6xy)dy\) between \(y=\sqrt x\) and \(y=x^2\): 2022–23 and 2024–25, and Lec 9 homework. Answer \(\tfrac32\).
  • \(\iint e^{xy}\,dA\) between \(y=x/2,\ y=x,\ y=1/x,\ y=2/x\): 2023–24 and 2024–25. Answer \(\tfrac{e(e-1)\ln 2}{2}\).
  • Work along \(x^2=4y,\ 3x^3=8z\): three papers. Answer 16.
  • \(u=e^{-x}(x\sin y-y\cos y)\): conjugate and f(z), three papers and the class "Exam" mark.
  • \(\cosh z=-2\): four papers. \(z^5=-32\), \(\int\bar z\,dz\) to \(4+2i\), \(\mathrm{Log}(-1+i\sqrt3)^2\): each two or three times.
  • PDE: 2023–24 and 2024–25 Q6 and Q7(c) are Farid sir's slide examples and exercises verbatim. Do every slide problem.

How to work through this book

  1. Each chapter = a few question types. Per type: recipe, what to write, traps, worked examples, then every past question.
  2. Solve each amber card on paper first. Then open the steps one at a time and find your first wrong line.
  3. Grade yourself ✓ / ~ / ✗. The chapter ticks itself in the sidebar when all mandatory cards are graded; ✗ and ~ go to the redo list.
  4. Grey-bordered cards are optional extras from older papers. Do them if the plan gives time.
