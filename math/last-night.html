Vector calculus

First topics, slowly: vectors, derivatives, space curves

  • Differentiate vectors componentwise. \((\vec A\times\vec B)'=\vec A\times\vec B'+\vec A'\times\vec B\) (order kept).
  • \(\vec v=\vec r'\), \(\vec a=\vec r''\), component along \(\vec p\) \(=\vec v\cdot\vec p/|\vec p|\).
  • Unit tangent \(\vec T=\vec r'/|\vec r'|\); tangent line \(\vec r_0+s\vec r'(t_0)\); \(ds/dt=|\vec r'|\).
  • \(\dfrac{d\vec T}{ds}=\kappa\vec N\), \(\vec B=\vec T\times\vec N\), \(\dfrac{d\vec B}{ds}=-\tau\vec N\); \(\kappa=\dfrac{|\vec r'\times\vec r''|}{|\vec r'|^3}\), \(\tau=\dfrac{(\vec r'\times\vec r'')\cdot\vec r'''}{|\vec r'\times\vec r''|^2}\).
  • Helix \((a\cos t,a\sin t,bt)\): \(\kappa=\dfrac a{a^2+b^2}\), \(\tau=\dfrac b{a^2+b^2}\). Circle: \(\kappa=1/a\), \(\tau=0\).
  • Osculating plane: through \(P\) with normal \(\vec r'\times\vec r''\).
  • \(a_T=\dfrac{\vec v\cdot\vec a}{|\vec v|}\), \(a_N=\dfrac{|\vec v\times\vec a|}{|\vec v|}=\sqrt{|\vec a|^2-a_T^2}=\kappa v^2\).

Gradient: tangent plane, directional derivative, angle between surfaces

  • \(\nabla\phi\perp\) level surface \(\phi=c\). Normal vector \(\vec n=\nabla F(P)\).
  • Tangent plane: \(F_x(x-x_0)+F_y(y-y_0)+F_z(z-z_0)=0\). Normal line: \(\frac{x-x_0}{F_x}=\frac{y-y_0}{F_y}=\frac{z-z_0}{F_z}\).
  • For \(z=f(x,y)\): plane \(f_x(x-x_0)+f_y(y-y_0)=z-z_0\).
  • \(D_{\hat a}\phi=\nabla\phi\cdot\hat a\), \(\hat a=\vec a/|\vec a|\). Max rate \(=|\nabla\phi|\) along \(\nabla\phi\).
  • \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{\nabla\phi_1\cdot\nabla\phi_2}{|\nabla\phi_1||\nabla\phi_2|}\); orthogonal if the dot product is 0.
  • Always check the point is on the surface first.

Divergence, curl, potentials and identities

  • \(\nabla\cdot\vec F=\sum\partial_iF_i\); curl by the determinant \(\hat i(\partial_yF_3-\partial_zF_2)-\hat j(\partial_xF_3-\partial_zF_1)+\hat k(\partial_xF_2-\partial_yF_1)\).
  • Solenoidal: div \(=0\). Irrotational: curl \(=\vec0\), then \(\vec F=\nabla\phi\).
  • Potential: integrate \(F_1\) in \(x\), \(F_2\) in \(y\), \(F_3\) in \(z\); union of distinct terms plus \(C\); check; work \(=\phi(B)-\phi(A)\).
  • \(\partial r/\partial x=x/r\); \(\nabla r^n=nr^{n-2}\vec r\); \(\nabla\cdot\vec r=3\); \(\nabla\times\vec r=\vec0\).
  • \(\nabla\cdot(r^n\vec r)=(n+3)r^n\): solenoidal iff \(n=-3\); \(f(r)\vec r\) solenoidal gives \(f=C/r^3\).
  • \(\nabla^2r^n=n(n+1)r^{n-2}\); \(\nabla^2(1/r)=0\); \(\vec E=\vec r/r^2\) has \(\varphi=\ln(r/a)\).
  • \(\nabla\times(\nabla\times\vec A)=-\nabla^2\vec A+\nabla(\nabla\cdot\vec A)\): compare \(\hat i\) components, \(\partial_{xy}A_2-\partial_{yy}A_1-\partial_{zz}A_1+\partial_{xz}A_3\).

Jacobian, Hessian, Laplacian and change of variables

  • \(\dfrac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}=x_uy_v-x_vy_u\); Hessian \(=[f_{x_ix_j}]\), \(D=f_{xx}f_{yy}-f_{xy}^2\); Laplacian \(=f_{xx}+f_{yy}+f_{zz}\).
  • \(\iint_R f\,dx\,dy=\iint_{R'} f\,\left|\dfrac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right|du\,dv\), and \(\dfrac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}=1\Big/\dfrac{\partial(u,v)}{\partial(x,y)}\).
  • Pick \(u,v\) so each is constant on a pair of boundary curves; \(R'\) is then a rectangle.
  • \(x\pm y=c\): \(u=x+y,\ v=x-y\), \(\partial(u,v)/\partial(x,y)=-2\). \(xy=c,\ y=mx\): \(u=xy,\ v=y/x\), \(\partial(u,v)/\partial(x,y)=2y/x=2v\).
  • Polar \(J=r\); \(x=au,y=bv\) \(J=ab\).
  • \(\iint e^{xy}dA=\dfrac{e(e-1)\ln2}{2}\approx1.619\); \(\iint\frac{x-y}{x+y}dA=\frac14\ln3\).

Line integrals and work done along a path

  • \(W=\int_C\vec F\cdot d\vec r=\int F_1dx+F_2dy+F_3dz\); put everything in terms of \(t\), do not forget \(dx=x'(t)\,dt\).
  • Segment \(P\to Q\): \(\vec r=P+t(Q-P)\), \(t:0\to1\). Constant coordinate means zero differential.
  • Intersection of surfaces (\(x^2=4y,\ 3x^3=8z\)): put \(x=t\), solve the others. Answer 16 for \(\vec F=(3x^2,\,2xz-y,\,z)\), \(x:0\to2\).
  • Circle \(x=a\cos t,\ y=a\sin t\); ellipse \(x=a\cos t,\ y=b\sin t\); \(t:0\to2\pi\) counter-clockwise.
  • Curl \(=\vec0\): \(\phi\) with \(\nabla\phi=\vec F\); \(W=\phi(B)-\phi(A)\); closed path gives 0.
  • Return legs have limits backwards. Final answers to remember: \(\frac{20}3\), 16, \(-6\), \(\frac{1763}{210}\), \(\frac\pi2-1\), \(18\pi\), \(96\pi\), \(-2\).

Green's theorem

  • \(\oint_CM\,dx+N\,dy=\iint_R(N_x-M_y)\,dA\), \(C\) counter-clockwise, \(R\) on the left. \(M\) goes with \(dx\), \(N\) with \(dy\).
  • Verify: sketch, label \(C_1,C_2,\dots\), arrows. Lower curve left to right, upper curve right to left. One integral per piece, then the double integral.
  • Repeat question: \((3x^2-8y^2)dx+(4y-6xy)dy\) between \(y=\sqrt x\) and \(y=x^2\): \(-1+\frac52=\frac32\) and \(\iint10y\,dA=\frac32\).
  • If \(N_x-M_y=k\) is constant, answer \(=k\cdot\)area. \((x-2y)dx+x\,dy\) on the unit circle \(=3\pi\).
  • Area \(=\frac12\oint(x\,dy-y\,dx)\); ellipse \(\pi ab\), astroid \(\frac{3\pi a^2}8\), polar \(\frac12\int r^2d\phi\).
  • Work round a parallelogram \((0,0),(2,0),(3,1),(1,1)\): \(-3\times2=-6\).

Surface integrals, volume integrals and the divergence theorem

  • Flux \(=\iint_S\vec F\cdot\hat n\,dS\), \(dS=\dfrac{dx\,dy}{|\hat n\cdot\hat k|}\) (or \(dy\,dz/|\hat n\cdot\hat i|\), \(dx\,dz/|\hat n\cdot\hat j|\)). Plane \(ax+by+cz=d\): \(\hat n\,dS=(a,b,c)\,dx\,dy/c\).
  • Vertical wall (cylinder): project onto \(xz\) or \(yz\), never \(xy\). Plane \(2x+3y+6z=12\), \(\vec A=(18z,-12,3y)\): 24. Cylinder \(x^2+y^2=16\): 90.
  • Gauss: \(\iint_S\vec F\cdot\hat n\,dS=\iiint_V\nabla\cdot\vec F\,dV\), \(\hat n\) outward, \(S\) closed.
  • \(\iint_S\vec r\cdot\hat n\,dS=3V\); \(\nabla\cdot(r^n\vec r)=(n+3)r^n\).
  • Verify: volume side plus one line per face (\(\pm\hat i,\pm\hat j,\pm\hat k\); cylinder wall \(dS=a\,d\theta\,dz\)). Bottom face has \(\hat n=-\hat k\).
  • Hemisphere \(\vec F\) with div \(=x^2+y^2+z^2\): \(\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^{\pi/2}\int_0^a\rho^4\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta=\frac{2\pi a^5}5\) (not \(\frac{2\pi a^5}3\)). Cylinder verify: \(84\pi\). Box verify: \(abc(a+b+c)\).

Stokes' theorem

  • \(\oint_C\vec F\cdot d\vec r=\iint_S(\nabla\times\vec F)\cdot\hat n\,dS\); \(C\) counter-clockwise seen from the tip of \(\hat n\) (surface on your left).
  • Curl \(=\hat i(\partial_yF_3-\partial_zF_2)-\hat j(\partial_xF_3-\partial_zF_1)+\hat k(\partial_xF_2-\partial_yF_1)\).
  • \(z=f(x,y)\), upward: \(\hat n\,dS=(-f_x,-f_y,1)\,dx\,dy\). Sphere: \(\hat n=\vec r/a\), \(dS=\frac az\,dx\,dy\). Flat in \(xy\): \(\hat n=\hat k\).
  • Same boundary, same value: replace a curved \(S\) by the flat disc.
  • Hemisphere \(\vec F=(2x-y,-yz^2,-y^2z)\): curl \(=\hat k\); both sides \(\pi\). Paraboloid \(\vec F=(2z,x,y^2)\): \(4\pi\). Rectangle \(\vec F=(x^2+y^2,-2xy,0)\): \(-4ab^2\).
  • Stokes flat in the plane is Green's theorem: \((\nabla\times\vec F)\cdot\hat k=N_x-M_y\).

Complex calculus

Complex numbers, roots, functions and mappings

  • \(z=re^{i\theta}\), \(\theta\in(-\pi,\pi]\); add \(\pm\pi\) to \(\tan^{-1}(b/a)\) in quadrants II, III.
  • \(z^{1/n}=r^{1/n}\operatorname{cis}\dfrac{\theta+2k\pi}{n}\), \(k=0..n-1\): regular \(n\)-gon of radius \(r^{1/n}\). \(z^5=-32\): \(2\operatorname{cis}\dfrac{(2k+1)\pi}5\).
  • \(w=u+iv\): put \(z=x+iy\), expand, separate; \(e^{iy}=\cos y+i\sin y\). For \(z^2\): \(u=x^2-y^2\), \(v=2xy\).
  • Under \(w=z^2\): \(x=c\mapsto u=c^2-\dfrac{v^2}{4c^2}\); \(y=c\mapsto u=\dfrac{v^2}{4c^2}-c^2\). Edges become parabolas.
  • Möbius image of a region: invert \(z=\dfrac{dw-b}{-cw+a}\), impose the condition, simplify, test one point. Three points to three points: cross-ratio.
  • \(\dfrac{z-i}{iz-1}\): \(\operatorname{Im}z\ge0\mapsto|w|\le1\). \(w=\dfrac{i(1-z)}{1+z}\): \(-i,0,i\mapsto-1,i,1\), the \(y\)-axis \(\mapsto|w|=1\).

Limits, continuity, differentiability and the Cauchy-Riemann equations

  • Limit: same value along every path. Does not exist if \(y=0\) and \(x=0\) (or \(y=x\)) disagree: \(\bar z/z\to1\) vs \(-1\).
  • ε-δ: \(\delta=\min(1,\varepsilon/K)\) after bounding \(|f-l|\lt K\delta\).
  • Continuous at \(z_0\): limit exists, \(f(z_0)\) defined, equal. \(\bar z^2/z\): \(|f|=|z|\to0\).
  • \(\dfrac{\Delta w}{\Delta z}\): along \(\Delta y=0\) gives \(\overline{\Delta z}/\Delta z=1\); along \(\Delta x=0\) gives \(-1\). \(|z|^2\): differentiable only at 0, \(f'(0)=0\), analytic nowhere.
  • C-R: \(u_x=v_y,\ u_y=-v_x\); \(f'=u_x+iv_x\). Sufficient at a point if partials are continuous. Analytic needs a neighbourhood.
  • Rectangular proof: \(\Delta y=0\Rightarrow f'=u_x+iv_x\); \(\Delta x=0\Rightarrow f'=v_y-iu_y\).
  • Polar C-R: \(u_r=\dfrac1rv_\theta,\ \ v_r=-\dfrac1ru_\theta\); \(f'=e^{-i\theta}(u_r+iv_r)=-\dfrac iz(u_\theta+iv_\theta)\).
  • Chain rule: \(u_x=u_r\cos\theta-\dfrac{u_\theta\sin\theta}r\), \(u_y=u_r\sin\theta+\dfrac{u_\theta\cos\theta}r\).

Analytic and harmonic functions, harmonic conjugate, \(f(z)\)

  • Harmonic: \(\psi_{xx}+\psi_{yy}=0\) (give the domain). Analytic: \(f'\) exists in a neighbourhood. Conjugate \(v\): \(u_x=v_y\), \(u_y=-v_x\).
  • \(u\) given: \(v=\int u_x\,dy+F(x)\); \(v_x=-u_y\) fixes \(F\). \(v\) given: \(u=\int v_y\,dx+G(y)\); \(u_y=-v_x\) fixes \(G\).
  • \(f(z)\): put \(y=0\), then \(x\to z\). Milne-Thomson: \(f'(z)=u_x(z,0)-iu_y(z,0)=v_y(z,0)+iv_x(z,0)\).
  • \(u=e^{-x}(x\sin y-y\cos y)\): \(v=e^{-x}(x\cos y+y\sin y)\), \(f=ize^{-z}\).
  • \(u=\tfrac12\log(x^2+y^2)\): \(v=\tan^{-1}(y/x)\), \(f=\log z\) (cut plane).
  • \(v=\dfrac x{x^2+y^2}+\cosh x\cos y\): \(u=\dfrac y{x^2+y^2}-\sinh x\sin y\), \(f=i\left(\cosh z+\dfrac1z\right)+c\).
  • Analytic \(\Rightarrow\) \(u,v\) harmonic (differentiate C-R, use \(v_{xy}=v_{yx}\)). Polar Laplace: \(u_{rr}+\dfrac1ru_r+\dfrac1{r^2}u_{\theta\theta}=0\). \(\nabla^2|f|^2=4|f'|^2\).

Elementary functions: exp, trig, log

  • \(\cos(x+iy)=\cos x\cosh y-i\sin x\sinh y\); \(\sin(x+iy)=\sin x\cosh y+i\cos x\sinh y\); \(\cosh(x+iy)=\cosh x\cos y+i\sinh x\sin y\).
  • \(\cosh t=a\) gives \(t=\pm\ln(a+\sqrt{a^2-1})\). Do not lose the \(\pm\).
  • \(\cosh z=-2\): \(z=\pm\ln(2+\sqrt3)+(2n+1)\pi i\). \(\cos z=4\): \(z=2n\pi\pm i\ln(4+\sqrt{15})\). \(\sin z=5\): \(z=(2n+\frac12)\pi\pm i\ln(5+2\sqrt6)\).
  • Principal \(\mathrm{Log}\,z=\ln|z|+i\theta\), \(-\pi<\theta\le\pi\). Multivalued \(\ln z=\ln r+i(\theta+2k\pi)\).
  • \(\mathrm{Log}(-1+i\sqrt3)^2=\ln4-\frac{2\pi}3i\ne2\,\mathrm{Log}(-1+i\sqrt3)=\ln4+\frac{4\pi}3i\). Always reduce the doubled angle.
  • \(z^c=e^{c\log z}\); \(|\cos z|^2=\cos^2x+\sinh^2y\); \(|\sin z|^2=\sin^2x+\sinh^2y\).

Complex line integrals

  • \(\int_Cf\,dz=\int f(z(t))\,z'(t)\,dt\). Segment: \(z=z_1+t(z_2-z_1)\), \(dz=(z_2-z_1)dt\). Circle: \(z=c+re^{i\theta}\), \(dz=ire^{i\theta}d\theta\).
  • \(\int_Cf\,dz=\int(u\,dx-v\,dy)+i\int(v\,dx+u\,dy)\).
  • Several pieces: add them. Reverse the direction: change the sign.
  • \(\int_C\bar z\,dz\), \(0\to4+2i\): \(10-\frac83i\) along \(z=t^2+it\); \(10-8i\) along \(0\to2i\to4+2i\).
  • \(\int dz/z\) on the unit semicircle from \(-1\) to \(1\): \(-\pi i\) (upper), \(+\pi i\) (lower). \(\oint_{|z|=1}dz/z=2\pi i\).
  • \(\oint\bar z\,dz=2i\times\text{(area enclosed)}\), anticlockwise. Triangle \(0,1+i,-2\): \(2i\).
  • ML: \(\left|\int_Cf\,dz\right|\le ML\). \(\left|\int_C\frac{dz}{z^2-1}\right|\le\frac\pi3\) on the first-quadrant arc of \(|z|=2\).

Cauchy's theorem, the integral formula, singular points

  • Cauchy-Goursat: \(f\) analytic in and on \(C\) \(\Rightarrow\oint_Cf\,dz=0\). Check every singular point is outside. \(\oint\operatorname{sech}z\,dz=0\) on \(|z|=1\) since \(\cosh z=0\) first at \(|z|=\tfrac\pi2\).
  • \(\oint_C\dfrac{dz}{z-a}=2\pi i\) if \(a\) is inside, \(0\) if outside.
  • CIF: \(\oint\dfrac{f(z)}{z-a}dz=2\pi i\,f(a)\); \(\oint\dfrac{f(z)}{(z-a)^{n+1}}dz=\dfrac{2\pi i}{n!}f^{(n)}(a)\).
  • Two points inside: partial fractions, \(\dfrac1{(z-1)(z-2)}=\dfrac1{z-2}-\dfrac1{z-1}\); then \(2\pi i\,[g(2)-g(1)]\).
  • Pole of order \(n\) at \(z_0\): \(\lim(z-z_0)^nf\) finite and \(\ne0\). Removable: \(\sin z/z\) at 0. At \(\infty\): study \(f(1/w)\).
  • Answers to know: \(\oint_{|z|=2}\frac{z\,dz}{(9-z^2)(z+i)}=\frac\pi5\); \(\oint_{|z|=3/2}\frac{4-3z}{z(z-1)(z-2)}=2\pi i\); \(\oint_{|z|=3}\frac{\sin\pi z^2+\cos\pi z^2}{(z-1)(z-2)}=4\pi i\); \(\frac1{2\pi i}\oint\frac{e^{zt}}{(z^2+1)^2}=\frac12(\sin t-t\cos t)\).
  • Not taught, in papers: \(\operatorname{Res}=\lim(z-a)f=p(a)/q'(a)\); \(\oint_{|z|=2}\frac{\coth z}{z-i}dz=2\pi(\cot1-1)\).

PDE

Forming a PDE: eliminate constants, eliminate functions

  • Constants: \(F_x+pF_z=0,\ F_y+qF_z=0\), eliminate \(a,b\). Three constants: go to second order and show all the second-order relations.
  • Ellipsoid-type \(\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}+\frac{z^2}{c^2}=1\) (and \(ax^2+by^2+cz^2=1\), hyper- and two-sheet versions): \(x(p^2+zr)=zp,\ y(q^2+zt)=zq\), equivalently \(pq+zs=0\).
  • Quadrics homogeneous in \(x,y\) of degree \(k\) (cone, paraboloids, \(z=ax^2+bxy+cy^2\)): \(xp+yq=kz\).
  • \(\phi(u,v)=0\): \(Pp+Qq=R\), \(P=u_yv_z-u_zv_y,\ Q=u_zv_x-u_xv_z,\ R=u_xv_y-u_yv_x\).
  • \(z=f(u)\): eliminate \(f'\) by dividing \(p\) by \(q\). \(z=f(x-at)+g(x+at)\): \(a^2z_{xx}=z_{tt}\). \(z=f(x^2-y)+g(x^2+y)\): \(xr-p=4x^3t\).

Lagrange's method: \(Pp+Qq=R\), and the integral surface through a curve

  • \(Pp+Qq=R\): auxiliary equations \(\dfrac{dx}P=\dfrac{dy}Q=\dfrac{dz}R\); two independent integrals \(u=c_1,v=c_2\); answer \(\phi(u,v)=0\).
  • Multiplier sets to try: \((1,1,1)\), \((x,y,z)\), \((\tfrac1x,\tfrac1y,\tfrac1z)\), \((y,x,-1)\), \((x,y,-1)\). Always check \(lP+mQ+nR=0\).
  • Second integral: use the first one to eliminate a variable (Method 3), a linear ODE, or a homogeneous ODE.
  • Surface through a curve: substitute the curve into \(u=c_1\), \(v=c_2\), eliminate the parameter to get \(G(c_1,c_2)=0\), then \(c_1\to u,\ c_2\to v\). Check the curve lies on it.
  • Correct slide answers: Application 1: \(x^2+y^2+2xyz-2z+2=0\). Application 3: \(z^4(x+y+z)^2+(y-x-z)^2-2z^4(x+y+z)+2z^2(y-x-z)=0\).
  • Past answers to know: 24-25 \(x+y^2+z^2+z=3\); 23-24 \((x-y)[(x+y+z-1)^2+(x+y)^2]=(x+y)(z^2-1)\); 22-23 \(x^2+y^2+z-xz-y=1\).

Non-linear first order: Charpit's method and the standard forms

  • Charpit: \(\dfrac{dp}{F_x+pF_z}=\dfrac{dq}{F_y+qF_z}=\dfrac{dz}{-pF_p-qF_q}=\dfrac{dx}{-F_p}=\dfrac{dy}{-F_q}\). Take the easiest integral \(G=a\), solve for \(p,q\), integrate \(dz=p\,dx+q\,dy\).
  • Quick shortcuts: \(F_x+pF_z=0\Rightarrow p=a\); \(\frac{dp}p=\frac{dq}q\Rightarrow p=aq\); \(p\,dp+q\,dq=0\Rightarrow p^2+q^2=a^2\).
  • Clairaut \(z=px+qy+f(p,q)\): \(z=ax+by+f(a,b)\); singular from \(x+f_a=0,\ y+f_b=0\).
  • \(f(z,p,q)=0\): \(q=ap\), \(u=x+ay\), \(dz/du=p(z)\); then \(\int dz/p(z)=x+ay+b\).
  • \(f_1(x,p)=f_2(y,q)=a\): \(z=\int p\,dx+\int q\,dy+b\). \(f(p,q)=0\): \(z=ax+\phi(a)y+c\), no singular.
  • Singular: eliminate \(a,b\) from \(f=f_a=f_b=0\) and check it satisfies the PDE. General: \(b=\phi(a)\), envelope in \(a\).
  • Past answers to know: \(px+qy=pq\): \(z=\frac{(ax+y)^2}{2a}+\frac ba\). \(2(z+px+qy)=yp^2\): \(z=\frac{ax}{y^2}+\frac by-\frac{a^2}{4y^3}\), no singular. \(2x(z^2q^2+1)=pz\): \(z^2=2(1+a^2)x^2+2ay+2b\).

Linear PDEs with constant coefficients: the complementary function

  • Homogeneous \(F\): \(D_x\to m,\ D_y\to1\), \(F(m,1)=0\); root \(m\) gives \(\phi(y+mx)\); a root repeated \(k\) times gives \(\phi_1+x\phi_2+\dots+x^{k-1}\phi_k\) (same argument); complex roots stay \(\phi(y\pm ix)\); factor \(D_y\) gives \(\phi(x)\).
  • Factor \((bD_x-aD_y)\) gives \(\phi(ax+by)\).
  • Non-homogeneous reducible: factor as \((L_1+a)(L_2+b)\) after matching degree-1 and constant terms.
  • Case 1: \((\alpha D_x+\beta D_y+\gamma)\to e^{-\gamma x/\alpha}\phi(\beta x-\alpha y)\). Case 2: \((\alpha D_x+\gamma)\to e^{-\gamma x/\alpha}\phi(y)\). Case 3: \((\beta D_y+\gamma)\to e^{-\gamma y/\beta}\phi(x)\).
  • \((D_x-mD_y-k)^r\to e^{kx}[\phi_1+x\phi_2+\dots+x^{r-1}\phi_r](y+mx)\).
  • Irreducible: \(z_c=\sum Ae^{hx+ky}\) with \(F(h,k)=0\) (\(D_x\to h,\ D_y\to k\)).
  • Number of arbitrary functions = order, for reducible operators.

Particular integrals and the full higher-order solutions

  • \(\frac1Fe^{ax+by}=\frac{e^{ax+by}}{F(a,b)}\). If \(F(a,b)=0\): factor, non-vanishing factors first by substitution, the vanishing factor last gives \(x\) (\(\frac{x^r}{r!}\) if repeated); or \(\frac{x\,e^{ax+by}}{F_{D_x}(a,b)}\), then \(\frac{x^2e}{F_{D_xD_x}}\).
  • \(\sin/\cos(ax+by)\): \(D_x^2\to-a^2,\ D_xD_y\to-ab,\ D_y^2\to-b^2\); odd remainder: multiply by the conjugate and replace again.
  • \(\phi(ax+by)\), homogeneous \(F\): \(\frac1{F(a,b)}\iint\phi\); failure: \(\frac1{(bD_x-aD_y)^r}\phi(ax+by)=\frac{x^r}{b^rr!}\phi\).
  • Polynomial: pull out \(D_x^n\) (homog.), the constant (non-homog.), or the lowest term (irreducible); expand \((1-u)^{-1}\) or \((1-u)^{-2}=1+2u+3u^2+\cdots\); \(\frac1{D_x}=\int dx\).
  • Shift: \(\frac1F e^{ax+by}V=e^{ax+by}\frac1{F(D_x+a,D_y+b)}V\); for \(V\cos(ax+by)\) shift by \(ia,ib\) and take Re (Im for \(\sin\)). Shift both \(D_x\) and \(D_y\).
  • General method: \(\frac1{D_x-mD_y}f=\int f(x,c-mx)dx\), \(c=y+mx\); with \(-k\): \(e^{kx}\int e^{-kx}f\,dx\). One factor at a time.
  • Always: \(z=z_c+z_p\); split the right side into pieces; omit nothing from the CF (\(n\) functions for an order-\(n\) reducible operator).

Euler–Cauchy PDEs (variable coefficients)

  • \(x=e^u,\ y=e^v:\quad xD_x=D_u,\ yD_y=D_v,\ x^2D_x^2=D_u(D_u-1),\ y^2D_y^2=D_v(D_v-1),\ xyD_xD_y=D_uD_v\).
  • Collect terms; the single-\(D\) terms usually cancel. Factor into \((aD_u+bD_v+c)\).
  • \((D_u-mD_v)\to\phi(x^my)\); \((D_u-mD_v-k)\to x^k\phi(x^my)\); \(D_u\to\phi(y)\); \(D_v\to\phi(x)\); \((D_v-k)\to y^k\phi(x)\); repeated factor adds \(\ln x\).
  • RHS \(x^\alpha y^\beta=e^{\alpha u+\beta v}\), PI \(=\dfrac{x^\alpha y^\beta}{F(\alpha,\beta)}\); \(\ln x=u,\ \ln y=v\); \(\sin(\ln x^2)=\sin2u\).
  • Failure \(F(a,b)=0\): \(\dfrac{v}{F'(a)}e^{av}\) (or shift and expand). Polynomial RHS: expand \(F^{-1}\) in a series.
  • \(yD_y^2\) alone: multiply by \(y\) first. Always finish in \(x,y\).

Classification of second-order PDEs

  • \(Au_{xx}+Bu_{xy}+Cu_{yy}+\dots\): \(B^2-4AC\gt0\) hyperbolic, \(=0\) parabolic, \(\lt0\) elliptic. \(B\) is the full coefficient of \(u_{xy}\).
  • Wave \(c^2u_{xx}-u_{tt}\): \(4c^2\gt0\) hyperbolic. Heat \(c^2u_{xx}-u_t\): 0 parabolic. Laplace: \(-4\) elliptic.
  • Variable coefficients: solve the inequality and name the regions (curve where it is 0 is parabolic).
  • Characteristic equation \(A\lambda^2-B\lambda+C=0\), \(\lambda=dy/dx\).
  • Hyperbolic \(\xi=y-\lambda_1x,\ \eta=y-\lambda_2x\to u_{\xi\eta}=\dots\); parabolic \(\xi=y-\lambda x,\ \eta=x\to u_{\eta\eta}=\dots\); elliptic \(\lambda=\alpha\pm i\beta\), \(\xi=y-\alpha x,\ \eta=\beta x\to u_{\xi\xi}+u_{\eta\eta}=\dots\).
  • Wave: \(\xi=x-ct,\ \eta=x+ct\to u_{\xi\eta}=0\), \(u=f(x-ct)+g(x+ct)\) (d'Alembert).

Separation of variables: wave, heat, Laplace

  • \(u=XT\), \(X''/X=\mu\). \(X(0)=X(L)=0\): \(\mu\gt0\to X\equiv0\) (exponentials); \(\mu=0\to X=Ax+B\equiv0\); \(\mu=-\lambda^2\to X=\sin\frac{n\pi x}L\), \(\lambda=\frac{n\pi}L\).
  • Wave: \(u=\sum\big(E_n\cos\frac{n\pi ct}L+F_n\sin\frac{n\pi ct}L\big)\sin\frac{n\pi x}L\); \(E_n=\frac2L\int_0^Lf\sin\); \(F_n=\frac2{n\pi c}\int_0^Lg\sin\).
  • Heat: \(u=\sum b_n\sin\frac{n\pi x}Le^{-c^2n^2\pi^2t/L^2}\), \(b_n=\frac2L\int_0^Lf\sin\). Non-zero ends: \(u=u_s+w\), \(u_s=T_1+(T_2-T_1)\frac xL\), \(w(x,0)=f-u_s\).
  • \(\int_0^L(Lx-x^2)\sin\frac{n\pi x}L=\frac{2L^3(1-(-1)^n)}{n^3\pi^3}\); \(\frac2L\int_0^Lx\sin\frac{n\pi x}L=\frac{2L(-1)^{n+1}}{n\pi}\); \(\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi(\pi-x)\sin nx=\frac2n\).
  • Finite sine sum as \(f\): match terms (\(\sin\pi x\) on \((0,2)\) is \(n=2\)). Insulated ends: cosine series with a constant term. Laplace: \(\sin\frac{n\pi x}a\sinh\frac{n\pi y}a\).
  • d'Alembert: \(u=\frac12[f(x-ct)+f(x+ct)]+\frac1{2c}\int_{x-ct}^{x+ct}g\).
  • Interpretation: standing waves \(\sin\frac{n\pi x}L\) with frequency \(\frac{nc}{2L}\) (wave); modes decay like \(e^{-c^2n^2\pi^2t/L^2}\) to the end temperature or steady state (heat).

Wave equation the O'Neil way

  • Problem: \(y_{tt}=c^2y_{xx}\), \(y(0,t)=y(L,t)=0\), \(y(x,0)=f\), \(y_t(x,0)=g\). Separate with \(X''/X=T''/c^2T=-\lambda\); \(\lambda_n=\frac{n^2\pi^2}{L^2}\), \(X_n=\sin\frac{n\pi x}L\).
  • \(y=\sum\big[a_n\cos\frac{n\pi ct}L+b_n\sin\frac{n\pi ct}L\big]\sin\frac{n\pi x}L\), \(a_n=\frac2L\int_0^Lf\sin\frac{n\pi x}L\), \(b_n=\frac2{n\pi c}\int_0^Lg\sin\frac{n\pi x}L\).
  • Rest: \(g=0\), only \(a_n\). Struck: \(f=0\), only \(b_n\) (with the \(\frac1{n\pi c}\)). Both: add. Finite sum of sines: match terms (\(\sin\pi x\) on \((0,4)\) is \(n=4\)).
  • Useful integrals: \(\int_0^\pi(\pi-x)\sin nx\,dx=\frac\pi n\); \(\int_0^L(Lx-x^2)\sin\frac{n\pi x}L=\frac{2L^3(1-(-1)^n)}{n^3\pi^3}\); pluck at \(a\), height \(h\): \(a_n=\frac{2hL^2\sin(n\pi a/L)}{\pi^2n^2a(L-a)}\).
  • Meaning: normal modes; fundamental frequency \(\frac c{2L}\); nodes at \(\frac{kL}n\); period \(\frac{2L}c\).
  • Forcing \(F(x)\): \(y=Y+\psi\), \(c^2\psi''+F=0\), \(\psi(0)=\psi(L)=0\); \(Y(x,0)=f-\psi\).
  • d'Alembert: \(u=\frac12[f(x-ct)+f(x+ct)]+\frac1{2c}\int_{x-ct}^{x+ct}g\,dw\) \(\big(+\frac1{2c}\int_0^t\int_{x-c(t-T)}^{x+c(t-T)}F\,dX\,dT\big)\).
