Green's theorem

State it, verify it (both sides), use it to get a line integral or an area. It came in all three MATH 241 papers: 2022-23 Q2(c),(d), 2023-24 Q2(c) (work round a parallelogram by Green) and 2024-25 Q2(c). The same verification question was set twice word for word.

Three types. (A) Verify Green's theorem: compute both sides and compare. (B) Evaluate a line integral using Green. (C) Area by a line integral. weak spot every year This is your slowest chapter: read the worked example slowly, then redo it with the page covered. 8 mandatory cards (the repeated question first), 12 optional.

1. The theorem

Green's theorem (write this word for word, 2 marks in 2022-23)
If \(R\) is a closed region of the \(xy\)-plane bounded by a simple closed curve \(C\), and \(M(x,y)\) and \(N(x,y)\) are continuous functions with continuous partial derivatives in \(R\), then \[\oint_C M\,dx+N\,dy=\iint_R\Big(\frac{\partial N}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial M}{\partial y}\Big)dx\,dy,\] where \(C\) is traversed in the positive (counter-clockwise) sense, so that \(R\) is on the left.
Vector form (Schaum 6.4)
\(\oint_C\vec F\cdot d\vec r=\iint_R(\nabla\times\vec F)\cdot\hat k\,dA\) with \(\vec F=M\hat i+N\hat j\).
Area
\(A=\frac12\oint_C(x\,dy-y\,dx)=\oint_C x\,dy=-\oint_C y\,dx\).

In words: a line integral round a closed curve equals the double integral over what it encloses of "\(N_x-M_y\)". Left side: a path, pieces, limits. Right side: one double integral. A verify question asks you to compute both and see they match.

Which letter is which. \(M\) is the coefficient of \(dx\), \(N\) the coefficient of \(dy\). Then \(N_x\) means "differentiate \(N\) with respect to \(x\)" and \(M_y\) means "differentiate \(M\) with respect to \(y\)". Do not swap.

2. Orientation: the thing that goes wrong

Counter-clockwise means the region is always on your left as you walk. For a region between a lower curve and an upper curve:

  • walk the lower curve from left to right;
  • walk the upper curve from right to left (backwards);
  • on a vertical edge, go up on the right edge and down on the left edge.

In every verify question, draw the region and put an arrow on each piece before writing a single integral. Name the pieces \(C_1,C_2,\dots\) and write the direction beside each one. Our class note lost a sign here: it integrated the parabola \(y^2=8x\) from \(-4\) to \(4\) instead of \(4\) to \(-4\) and got \(-\frac85\) instead of \(+\frac85\).

3. Type A: verify Green's theorem every year

Recipe · verify Green's theorem

  1. Sketch the region. Find where the curves meet (set the equations equal). Mark the counter-clockwise direction and name each boundary piece \(C_1,C_2,\dots\).
  2. Read off \(M\) (coefficient of \(dx\)) and \(N\) (coefficient of \(dy\)). Write down \(N_x\) and \(M_y\).
  3. Line integral, piece by piece. For each \(C_i\): choose a parameter (\(x\), \(y\) or \(t\)), write \(dx,dy\) in it, give the limits in the direction of travel, integrate. Write the value of each piece. Add them.
  4. Double integral. Compute \(N_x-M_y\). Set limits from the picture (vertical strips: \(y\) from the lower curve to the upper curve, \(x\) over the shadow). Integrate.
  5. Compare: "\(\therefore\) L.H.S. \(=\) R.H.S. Green's theorem is verified."

What to write (12 marks)

(1) The statement of Green's theorem (see section 1). (2) Figure with the region, counter-clockwise arrows, \(C_1,C_2\) labelled and the intersection points. (3) \(M=\dots,\ N=\dots\). (4) One short integral per piece, with limits, and its value. (5) The sum \(=\) L.H.S. (6) \(N_x-M_y\), the limits, the double integral and its value \(=\) R.H.S. (7) The closing line "L.H.S. \(=\) R.H.S., verified".

Traps

  • Return arc run forwards (sign flip). Lower curve goes left to right; upper curve goes right to left.
  • Using the wrong region. "Square \(x=\pm1,\ y=\pm1\)" is \([-1,1]^2\), not \([0,1]^2\) (the class note got \(\frac12\) instead of 0).
  • Mixing up \(M_y\) with \(M_x\), or using \(dx\) where the piece has \(dx=0\).
  • When \(N_x-M_y\) has a part odd in \(y\) over a region symmetric about the \(x\)-axis, that part is 0. Spot it and save a page.
  • Forgetting the \(dy=f'(x)\,dx\) factor when you turn a piece into one variable.

4. Type B: evaluate a line integral using Green

When the question says "use Green's theorem to find \(\oint\dots\)" (or "find the work done round ..."), you skip the line integral. Compute \(N_x-M_y\) and integrate it over the region.

Recipe · evaluate by Green

  1. Check the curve is closed and counter-clockwise (if it is clockwise, put a minus sign in front).
  2. \(M\) = coefficient of \(dx\), \(N\) = coefficient of \(dy\). Compute \(N_x-M_y\).
  3. If \(N_x-M_y\) is a constant \(k\): answer \(=k\times\) area of the region (disc \(\pi r^2\), ellipse \(\pi ab\), parallelogram \(|\vec u\times\vec v|\), triangle \(\frac12|\vec u\times\vec v|\)).
  4. Otherwise set up the double integral: rectangles and squares in \(x,y\); discs and quarter-discs in polar coordinates (\(x=r\cos\theta,\ y=r\sin\theta,\ dA=r\,dr\,d\theta\)).
  5. Box the value.

What to write

The statement of Green's theorem in one line, \(M,N\), \(N_x-M_y\), the region and limits, the integral, the boxed value. Do not parametrise the curve.

Traps

  • A 3D field in a plane question: put \(z=0,\ dz=0\) first, then use \(M\) and \(N\) only.
  • Clockwise curves: the answer changes sign.
  • Forgetting the extra \(r\) in \(dA=r\,dr\,d\theta\).

5. Type C: area by a line integral

Recipe · area of a curve given parametrically

  1. Use \(A=\frac12\oint(x\,dy-y\,dx)\) (this is Green with \(M=-y\), \(N=x\), so \(N_x-M_y=2\)).
  2. Parametrise the curve counter-clockwise: \(x=x(t),\ y=y(t)\). Then \(x\,dy-y\,dx=(x\dot y-y\dot x)\,dt\).
  3. \(A=\frac12\int(x\dot y-y\dot x)\,dt\) over one full trip.
  4. For polar curves \(r=f(\phi)\): \(x\,dy-y\,dx=r^2d\phi\), so \(A=\frac12\int r^2d\phi\) (Schaum 6.45).

Trap

If your parametrisation runs clockwise (as the cycloid arch does), the integral comes out negative: the area is the absolute value.

One-minute summary

  • \(\oint_CM\,dx+N\,dy=\iint_R(N_x-M_y)\,dA\), \(C\) counter-clockwise, \(R\) on the left. \(M\) goes with \(dx\), \(N\) with \(dy\).
  • Verify: sketch, label \(C_1,C_2,\dots\), arrows. Lower curve left to right, upper curve right to left. One integral per piece, then the double integral.
  • Repeat question: \((3x^2-8y^2)dx+(4y-6xy)dy\) between \(y=\sqrt x\) and \(y=x^2\): \(-1+\frac52=\frac32\) and \(\iint10y\,dA=\frac32\).
  • If \(N_x-M_y=k\) is constant, answer \(=k\cdot\)area. \((x-2y)dx+x\,dy\) on the unit circle \(=3\pi\).
  • Area \(=\frac12\oint(x\,dy-y\,dx)\); ellipse \(\pi ab\), astroid \(\frac{3\pi a^2}8\), polar \(\frac12\int r^2d\phi\).
  • Work round a parallelogram \((0,0),(2,0),(3,1),(1,1)\): \(-3\times2=-6\).
