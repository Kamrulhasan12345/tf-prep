Term final 2018–19

210 marks, 3 hours, two sections, answer any three of four in each (about 0.85 minutes per mark). Section A (combinational) Q1–Q4, Section B (sequential) Q5–Q8; separate scripts. All tables and figures below were re-read from the scanned paper.

If this were your paper, pick these.
Section A: Q1, Q3, Q4. Q1 (35): PLA, two-level forms, K-map and a simplification are all procedures you rehearse. Q3 (35): decoder design, priority encoder and decoder-as-demux. Q1(b) · 6 marks · NAND-AND and OR-NAND forms

Express f(A,B,C,D) = Σ(0,4,8,9,10,11,12,14) in the two-level forms NAND-AND and OR-NAND.

Answer

Time: about 5 min

Both forms give the complement of a normal two-level circuit, so feed them the simplified F'. Zeros of f: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 13, 15.

  1. F' as SOP (K-map of the zeros): F' = A'D + A'C + BD. NAND-AND: first-level NAND per product term, second-level AND: AND of (term)' = (sum of terms)' = F.
  2. F' as POS (K-map of the 1s, sums): F' = (C + D)(A' + B)(A' + D). OR-NAND: first-level OR per sum term, second-level NAND: (product of sums)' = F.

Both circuits checked against all 16 minterms.

Q5(b) · 8 marks · master-slave J-K with NAND gates

Draw a master-slave J-K flip-flop using only NAND gates.

Answer

Time: about 7 min

  1. Master: two 3-input NANDs take (J, Q', CLK) and (K, Q, CLK); their outputs feed a cross-coupled NAND pair (an S'R' latch) with outputs M and M'.
  2. Slave: two 2-input NANDs take (M, CLK') and (M', CLK') and feed a second cross-coupled NAND pair with outputs Q and Q'. CLK' is a NAND used as an inverter.
  3. Q and Q' go back to the master input gates: this feedback makes J = K = 1 toggle and, because the master is closed while the slave is open, prevents race-around.
  4. Operation: CLK = 1 → master follows J, K, slave frozen; CLK falls → master frozen, slave copies M.

Q7(c) · 7 marks · S = R = 1 in an SR latch

Discuss why S = R = 1 leads to an unstable condition for an SR latch.

Answer

Time: about 6 min

  1. NOR latch, S = R = 1: both NOR outputs are forced to 0, so Q = Q' = 0, which breaks the rule Q' = complement of Q (an invalid state).
  2. Stable while it lasts, but when S and R both return to 0, each NOR sees two 0 inputs and tries to switch to 1; each 1 fed to the other NOR would force it to 0.
  3. This is a race: the gate with the shorter delay wins, so the final state (Q = 0 or 1) is unpredictable; with equal delays the latch oscillates.
  4. Hence S = R = 1 is forbidden (NAND latch: S' = R' = 0 is forbidden for the same reason).
