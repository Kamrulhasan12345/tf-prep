DBMS Notes

Textbook-style explanations of every lecture slide, with drawn figures, step-through animations, interactive tools, practice quizzes, and worked answers to the questions from both question banks.

How to use this book

  • Contents: the sidebar on the left (the Contents menu on a phone). Click a lecture to open its chapters.
  • Animation figures have Prev / Next / Play buttons. Click one, then use the arrow keys.
  • Interactive tools take your own input (FDs, schedules, logs, block counts) and solve it step by step. Use them to check your homework.
  • Every chapter ends with Practice: flip cards, a quiz that explains each answer, type-in problems, and Exam questions from the question banks with collapsible worked answers.
  • Chapters marked exam cover topics the slides skip but past exams ask about.
  • SQL playgrounds (Ishrak sir's Lec 02–12) run your SQL live in the browser. They accept Oracle syntax and need internet the first time.
  • AI check my answer: every exam question has a box where you type your answer, and the AI marks it point by point against the worked answer. In the ER chapters you can also answer with an ER diagram: type it in the converter's text format, watch it draw, and get it marked. To also upload a photo of a handwritten answer, run this book from your own computer and start the helper with python3 tools/ai_proxy.py (from this folder).
